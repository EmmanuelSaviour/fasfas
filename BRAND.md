## Official Brand Decisions (v1)

- Logo direction: Concept C (Footprint transforming into leaves)
- Primary colors: Emerald Green, Forest Green
- Accent color: Warm Gold
- Typography: Poppins + Inter
- Visual theme: Nature, growth, simplicity
- Brand feeling: Users should feel seen, growing, proud, and supported.


## Growth is Earned

At FasFas, growth cannot be bought.

Every member begins with the same seed.

Every tree grows through consistency, discipline, and verified movement.

No subscription, purchase, or upgrade can accelerate or replace that journey.

Your tree is a reflection of your commitment.

---

## FasFas+

FasFas+ enhances expression, not progression.

Premium members can personalize how their journey is presented while preserving the fairness of the platform.

Examples include:

* Seasonal themes
* Decorative pots and planters
* Landscape backgrounds
* Leaf color themes
* Weather effects
* Celebration animations
* Profile customization
* Exclusive app themes

These customizations never change:

* Tree growth
* Rank progression
* Streaks
* Verified runs
* Achievements

Every user earns growth in exactly the same way.

# The FasFas Constitution

Every decision we make must honor these principles.

1. Growth is earned.
2. Consistency is celebrated.
3. Users are encouraged, never shamed.
4. Simplicity beats complexity.
5. Nature is our visual language.
6. Community is built through shared growth.
7. Every feature must feel like FasFas.
