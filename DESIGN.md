# FasFas Design System

## Primary Colors

Emerald Green

Represents:
- Growth
- Nature
- Progress

Blue

Represents:
- Trust
- Stability
- Community

White

Represents:
- Simplicity
- Clarity

Never harsh.

## Animations

Smooth.

Natural.

Fast.

Nothing flashy.

## Typography

Clear.

Bold.

Easy to read.

## Design Philosophy

Every screen should feel calm.

Every interaction should encourage movement.

Every page should reinforce growth.
