Project: FasFas

1. Core Philosophy

FasFas is not simply a running tracker. It is a community built around movement, consistency, growth, and long-term participation.

Core principles:

Keep Moving.

Keep Growing.

Reward consistency, not perfection.

Small actions create meaningful growth.

Individual consistency creates a community forest.

One runner. One tree. One community.

2. Official Rank System

There are four ranks, with 10 additional verified runs required for each rank:

Rank    Journey

Chaser  First 10 verified runs

Runner  Next 10 verified runs

Tracker Next 10 verified runs

Stubborn    Final 10-run journey

Progression:

Chaser

  ↓ 10 verified runs

Runner

  ↓ 10 verified runs

Tracker

  ↓ 10 verified runs

Stubborn

  ↓ final 10-run journey

Permanent Flourished Tree

A run only counts after it is verified.

3. Consistency Tree

The Consistency Tree is a defining FasFas feature. It represents the member's consistency journey rather than being simple decoration.

During each 10-run rank journey, the tree grows through five visual stages:

🌱 Seed

  ↓

🌿 Sprout

  ↓

🌱 Young Tree

  ↓

🌳 Growing

  ↓

🌳 Flourishing

The exact run-to-stage thresholds will be connected to verified-run data later.

When the member completes the 10-run journey and earns the next rank, the tree starts a new cycle from Seed.

4. Stubborn / Permanent Tree

Stubborn is the final rank.

After the member completes the final required 10-run journey and reaches the permanent-tree milestone, the tree does not reset.

It becomes:

🌳 PERMANENTLY FLOURISHED

This is the member's visible symbol of lasting consistency and legacy.

5. Community Forest

Every member has an individual tree. Collectively, those trees form the FasFas Community Forest.

Every run grows your tree. Every tree grows our forest.

The existing Community Forest landing-page concept will eventually connect to real member trees.

6. Dashboard

The authenticated Dashboard is the private home of a FasFas member.

It includes:

FasFas logo

2–3 → Sprout

6–7 → Growing

Long-term consistency
